I never felt particularly proud of myself.
I was much less than perfect. I made mistakes,
and decisions I regret. I was not always
thoughtful and deliberate; not always fully aware.
I didn't live up to these aspirations,
even if I always meant well, and never ill.

You need not know me, or my story.
And if you already do, that knowledge
is surely tainted by half-truths.
It does not matter.
I dare not hope that it does.
You may believe what you choose to,
my words among them or not.

I pray only that it meant something.
And that you permit that the good remains.
