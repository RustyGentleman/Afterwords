# Grief

tacIt is an odd thing, to grieve your own death.

To grieve a possible future that will never come to pass; the things you still wanted to do before dying. To grieve the chances you'll never have to meet up with the people you'll never come to know, as well as the ones you have. To grieve... the _non-life_ of the daughter you **always** wanted to have.

As I felt my time draw near, even though it had been most of a year since I'd made the decision, _even_ though I'd been medicated, it... still felt like what I should do. It still felt like the only exit out of a corridor of misery.

tacAnd despite that, I... still grieved.

tacMy heart still ached.

tacI still wanted to live.

tacI just... couldn't do it.
